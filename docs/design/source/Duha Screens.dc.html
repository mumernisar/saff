1G · Alarm ringing, saff of 3 or more
The peek gives way to the row itself. Everyone stands on one line: people who are up fill their place with their colour and open eyes, people still asleep are dashed gaps snoring z’s with a Nudge under them. You are the gold face in the corner: drag yourself into your dashed spot at the end of the row and it snaps in, then the morning blooms. Past five people the row keeps the first one up and the sleepers, and folds the rest into “+8 others” with one Nudge for all of them.
